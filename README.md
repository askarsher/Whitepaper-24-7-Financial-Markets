# Whitepaper-24-7-Financial-Markets
This is a 3-part series of research notes which was written by me and Jakob Eduard Laul for Prometheus Capital UvA in 2026. We cover the eminence of 24/7 market platforms like QFEX and the subsequent shift towards continuous trading from Nasdaq and NYSE. The third and final paper investigates the consequences of longer trading windows - and just as importantly, shorter night sessions - for quants and trading models.
